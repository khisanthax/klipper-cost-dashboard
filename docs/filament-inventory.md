# Filament inventory

Inventory records physical spools independently of filament pricing profiles and print history. It is available in SQL-only mode.

1. Open **Inventory**, add a spool, and enter the net weight and its measured opening balance in grams. Purchase date, purchase price, and verified grams per meter are optional. A low-stock threshold shows a badge when the balance reaches it.
2. Assign a spool to a registered printer if useful. This assignment identifies where the spool is kept. A job may use several physical spools.
3. For a completed, failed, or canceled history job, open **Record verified job use** on the relevant spool. Select or enter the job UID, enter the measured grams used from that spool, and confirm the measurement. The list suggests the 100 most recent terminal jobs. Older jobs can be entered by UID.
4. Use **Record estimated job use** to associate a spool with a saved SQL job UID and retain an estimated gram quantity and its source, such as a slicer report or length × verified grams/meter. Saving or revising an estimate never changes the physical balance. The latest estimate is shown separately from measured use; earlier entries remain in SQL. Normal live print state has no stable job UID until terminal logging; active live and planned-item integration is deferred until it can preserve that identity without filename guesses.
5. If an existing job measurement was wrong, open **Verified consumption and corrections**, enter the corrected total grams for that spool and job, and give a reason. A zero total can reverse an erroneous deduction. The balance changes by the difference, and the original event remains linked to the correction. A form opened before another correction is rejected so it cannot overwrite a newer measurement.
6. Use **Adjust stock** for a measured stock change unrelated to a particular job, such as a corrected opening balance. Enter a signed quantity and the reason.
7. **Archive spool** retires it with a reason and preserves its remaining balance, purchase information and history. Reactivate it before assigning it or recording further stock changes. Spools with audit history are retained rather than hard-deleted.

Balances and changes are stored to the nearest supported 0.001 g. A transaction updates the spool and its stock ledger together. Repeating the same measured deduction for a spool and job is a no-op. A different quantity for the same spool and job requires a linked correction. Corrections, estimates, archival, adjustments and spool creation protect against replay of the same submitted form. Replayed forms explicitly report that stock was not changed again. Corrections cannot overdraw stock or exceed the spool's original net weight.

The history job and its costs are unaffected by inventory changes. Job deletion preserves previously recorded estimates, stock events and their captured job UID and filename. It does not automatically restore stock. A linked correction remains possible after that job is deleted. Estimates, unknown usage and ordinary print events do not deduct stock. The stock history shows the last 100 events, including opening balance, adjustments, verified job deductions and linked corrections. Each spool retains its own purchase record. The UI shows the 100 most recent verified uses and latest job estimates for each spool; complete records remain in SQL and backups.

SQLite backups contain spool balances, stock events, linked corrections, estimates and archival events. Migration `0010_filament_inventory` creates the base tables. Additive migration `0011_inventory_recovery` preserves already-installed `0010` balances and history. Use the standard coordinated backup and isolated restore procedure before upgrading; tests verify exact inventory-table restoration together with database integrity and foreign keys. No file outside the canonical SQL database is required for inventory recovery.
